Context:
On Tuesday Vladimir and Wendy each eat exactly four separate meals: breakfast, lunch, dinner, and a snack. The following is all that is known about what they eat during that day: At no meal does Vladimir eat the same kind of food as Wendy. Neither of them eats the same kind of food more than once during the day. For breakfast, each eats exactly one of the following: hot cakes, poached eggs, or omelet. For lunch, each eats exactly one of the following: fish, hot cakes, macaroni, or omelet. For dinner, each eats exactly one of the following: fish, hot cakes, macaroni, or omelet. For a snack, each eats exactly one of the following: fish or omelet. Wendy eats an omelet for lunch.

Question: Vladimir must eat which one of the following foods?

Options: 
A) fish
B) hot cakes
C) macaroni
D) omelet
E) poached eggs

Reasoning:
Since Wendy eats omelet for lunch, Vladimir cannot eat omelet for lunch. Given the constraints that neither person eats the same food twice and they never eat the same food at the same meal, let's analyze what Vladimir must eat. For snack, the only options are fish or omelet. If Vladimir doesn't eat omelet anywhere, he must eat fish for snack. But then he can't eat fish for lunch or dinner. Looking at breakfast, Vladimir could eat hot cakes, poached eggs, or omelet. If he eats omelet for breakfast, he satisfies the constraint of eating omelet. Since each person must eat different foods and Wendy already has omelet at lunch, Vladimir must eat omelet at some meal to ensure variety. Therefore, Vladimir must eat omelet.

The correct option is: D
