2. Variables:

    - Constraint 3 (Guitarist not 4th): `position[0] != 4`
    - Constraint 4 (Percussionist before Keyboard): `position[2] < position[1]`
    - Constraint 5 (Violinist before Keyboard before Guitarist): `And(position[5] < position[1], position[1] < position[0])`
    - Constraint 6 (Saxophonist after Percussionist or Trumpeter, not both): `Or(And(position[2] < position[3], Not(position[4] < position[3])), And(Not(position[2] < position[3]), position[4] < position[3]))`
