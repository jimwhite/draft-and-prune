    - Constraint 3 (More on bottom than top): `Sum([If(book_shelf[b] == 2, 1, 0) for b in range(8)]) > Sum([If(book_shelf[b] == 0, 1, 0) for b in range(8)])`

    - Constraint 5 (K higher than F): `book_shelf[4] < book_shelf[0]`

    - Constraint 7 (F and M same shelf): `book_shelf[0] == book_shelf[6]`
    - Constraint 8 (L higher than H): `book_shelf[5] < book_shelf[2]`

4. Answering the Question:

    - For each answer choice:
