    -   Constraint 5 (Pharmacy next to O or V): `ForAll([i], Implies(And(i >= 1, i <= 7, business_at_space[i] == 1), Or(And(i > 1, Or(business_at_space[i-1] == 0, business_at_space[i-1] == 6)), And(i < 7, Or(business_at_space[i+1] == 0, business_at_space[i+1] == 6)))))`

    -   Constraint 7 (Shoe store in space 4): `business_at_space[4] == 4`
