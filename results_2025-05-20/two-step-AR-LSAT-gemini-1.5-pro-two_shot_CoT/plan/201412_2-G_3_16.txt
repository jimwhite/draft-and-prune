    - Constraint 5 (Olive Rule): ForAll `r` in range(3), Implies(color_in_rug[O] == r, color_in_rug[P] == r) # If olive is in a rug, peach is also in that rug.

    - Constraint 7 (Peach/Turquoise Rule): ForAll `r` in range(3), Not(And(color_in_rug[P] == r, color_in_rug[T] == r)) # Peach and turquoise are not in the same rug.
    - Constraint 8 (Peach/Yellow Rule): ForAll `r` in range(3), Not(And(color_in_rug[P] == r, color_in_rug[Y] == r)) # Peach and yellow are not in the same rug.
    - Constraint 9 (Num Colors in Rug Calculation): ForAll `r` in range(3), num_colors_in_rug[r] == Sum([If(color_in_rug[c] == r, 1, 0) for c in range(6)]) # Calculate the number of colors in each rug.
    - Constraint 10 (Solid Yellow Rug): Exists `r` in range(3), And(color_in_rug[Y] == r, num_colors_in_rug[r] == 1) # One rug is solid yellow.
