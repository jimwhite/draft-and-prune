2.  Variables:

3.  Constraints:

    *   Constraint 2 (White Rule): `r = Int('r')`. `ForAll([r], Implies(rug_colors[r][W], Sum([If(rug_colors[r][c], 1, 0) for c in range(6)]) == 3))`
    *   Constraint 3 (Olive Rule): `r = Int('r')`. `ForAll([r], Implies(rug_colors[r][O], rug_colors[r][P]))`
    *   Constraint 4 (Forest/Turquoise Rule): `r = Int('r')`. `ForAll([r], Not(And(rug_colors[r][F], rug_colors[r][T])))`
    *   Constraint 5 (Peach/Turquoise Rule): `r = Int('r')`. `ForAll([r], Not(And(rug_colors[r][P], rug_colors[r][T])))`
    *   Constraint 6 (Peach/Yellow Rule): `r = Int('r')`. `ForAll([r], Not(And(rug_colors[r][P], rug_colors[r][Y])))`
