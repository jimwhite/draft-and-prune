Revised Plan:
1.  Entities:
    - Movies: Horror (H=0), Mystery (M=1), Romance (R=2), Sci-Fi (S=3), Western (W=4)
    - Screens: Screen 1 (1), Screen 2 (2), Screen 3 (3)
    - Times: 7 PM (7), 8 PM (8), 9 PM (9)

2.  Variables:
    - `movie_screen = Array('movie_screen', IntSort(), IntSort())` - Maps movie ID to screen ID.
    - `movie_time = Array('movie_time', IntSort(), IntSort())` - Maps movie ID to time.

3.  Constraints:
    - Constraint 1 (Screen Assignment): For all `m` in range(5), `Or(movie_screen[m] == 1, movie_screen[m] == 2, movie_screen[m] == 3)` - Each movie is shown on one of the three screens.
    - Constraint 2 (Time Assignment): For all `m` in range(5), `Or(movie_time[m] == 7, movie_time[m] == 8, movie_time[m] == 9)` - Each movie starts at 7, 8, or 9 PM.
    - Constraint 3 (Distinct Movie Assignments to Slots): For any two distinct movies `m1` and `m2` (where `m1 != m2`), it must be true that `Or(movie_screen[m1] != movie_screen[m2], movie_time[m1] != movie_time[m2])`. This ensures no two movies are assigned to the same (screen, time) slot.
    - Constraint 4 (Screen 1 and 2 Capacity): `Sum([If(movie_screen[m] == 1, 1, 0) for m in range(5)]) == 2` and `Sum([If(movie_screen[m] == 2, 1, 0) for m in range(5)]) == 2` - Screens 1 and 2 show two movies each.
    - Constraint 5 (Screen 3 Capacity): `Sum([If(movie_screen[m] == 3, 1, 0) for m in range(5)]) == 1` - Screen 3 shows one movie.
    - Constraint 6 (Screen 1 and 2 Time Slots): For all `t` in [7, 9], `Sum([If(And(movie_screen[m] == 1, movie_time[m] == t), 1, 0) for m in range(5)]) == 1` and `Sum([If(And(movie_screen[m] == 2, movie_time[m] == t), 1, 0) for m in range(5)]) == 1` - Screens 1 and 2 have a movie at 7 PM and 9 PM.
    - Constraint 7 (Screen 3 Time Slot): `Sum([If(And(movie_screen[m] == 3, movie_time[m] == 8), 1, 0) for m in range(5)]) == 1` - Screen 3 has a movie at 8 PM.
    - Constraint 8 (Western before Horror): `movie_time[W] < movie_time[H]`
    - Constraint 9 (Sci-fi not on Screen 3): `movie_screen[S] != 3`
    - Constraint 10 (Romance not on Screen 2): `movie_screen[R] != 2`
    - Constraint 11 (Horror and Mystery different screens): `movie_screen[H] != movie_screen[M]`
    - Constraint 12 (Romance before Western): `movie_time[R] < movie_time[W]` (This is the additional premise from the question)

            - D: `Not(movie_time[S] == 9)`
            - E: `Not(movie_screen[S] == 2)`
        - Check `solver.check()`.
        - If `solver.check() == unsat`, it means the negation is impossible, so the original answer choice must be true. Print the corresponding letter and stop.
        - Pop the solver state.
