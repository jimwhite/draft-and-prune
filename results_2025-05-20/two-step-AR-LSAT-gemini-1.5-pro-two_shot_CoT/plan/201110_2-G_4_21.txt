2. Variables:

        - `middle_count = Sum([If(book_shelf[i] == 1, 1, 0) for i in range(8)])`
        - `bottom_count = Sum([If(book_shelf[i] == 2, 1, 0) for i in range(8)])`
    - Constraint 2 (Total books): `top_count + middle_count + bottom_count == 8`
    - Constraint 3 (At least two books per shelf): `And(top_count >= 2, middle_count >= 2, bottom_count >= 2)`
    - Constraint 4 (More books on bottom than top): `bottom_count > top_count`
    - Constraint 5 (I on middle shelf): `book_shelf[3] == 1`
    - Constraint 6 (K higher than F): `book_shelf[4] < book_shelf[0]`
    - Constraint 7 (O higher than L): `book_shelf[7] < book_shelf[5]`
    - Constraint 8 (F and M on same shelf): `book_shelf[0] == book_shelf[6]`

4. Answering the Question:

    - For each answer choice:

        - Add the constraint corresponding to the *negation* of the answer choice:

            - B: Negation of 'K is placed on a shelf higher than the shelf G is placed on' (`book_shelf[4] < book_shelf[1]`) is `book_shelf[4] >= book_shelf[1]`.
