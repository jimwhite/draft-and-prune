1. Entities:

    - Screens: Screen 1 (1), Screen 2 (2), Screen 3 (3)
    - Times: 7 PM (7), 8 PM (8), 9 PM (9)

2. Variables:

    - `movie_time = Array('movie_time', IntSort(), IntSort())` - Maps movie ID to time.

    - Constraint 3 (Screen 2, 7 PM slot): `Sum([If(And(movie_screen[m] == 2, movie_time[m] == 7), 1, 0) for m in range(5)]) == 1`
    - Constraint 4 (Screen 2, 9 PM slot): `Sum([If(And(movie_screen[m] == 2, movie_time[m] == 9), 1, 0) for m in range(5)]) == 1`
    - Constraint 5 (Screen 3, 8 PM slot): `Sum([If(And(movie_screen[m] == 3, movie_time[m] == 8), 1, 0) for m in range(5)]) == 1`
    - Constraint 6 (Western before Horror): `movie_time[W] < movie_time[H]`
    - Constraint 7 (Sci-fi not on Screen 3): `movie_screen[S] != 3`
    - Constraint 8 (Romance not on Screen 2): `movie_screen[R] != 2`
    - Constraint 9 (Horror and Mystery different screens): `movie_screen[H] != movie_screen[M]`

4. Answering the Question:
    - Create a base Z3 solver with constraints 1-9.
    - For each answer choice (list of two movies for Screen 1, 7 PM then 9 PM):
        - Push the solver state.
        - Add temporary constraints based on the choice. For a choice `[movie_A, movie_B]` (using movie IDs):

            - `movie_screen[movie_B] == 1` and `movie_time[movie_B] == 9`
        - Check `solver.check()`. If `unsat`, this choice is impossible. This is the correct answer. Print the choice and stop.
        - Pop the solver state.
