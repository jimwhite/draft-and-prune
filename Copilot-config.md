## Model Recommendations

Based on the paper's generalization experiments (Section on additional models, AR-LSAT, k=10):

| Model | CoT Baseline | D&P Score | D&P Lift | Why Interesting |
|-------|-------------|-----------|----------|-----------------|
| **DeepSeek-R1** | 83.0% | **93.8%** | +10.8pp | Best absolute D&P score of any model tested; strong reasoning model that still benefits significantly from the framework |
| **GLM-4-plus** | 49.1% | **80.0%** | +30.9pp | Largest relative improvement of any model — demonstrates D&P lifts weaker models dramatically |

For LM Studio, the most practical addition would be a **DeepSeek-R1 distillation** (e.g., `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B` or the 14B variant if VRAM-constrained). The paper shows it achieves the highest absolute D&P accuracy (93.8% on AR-LSAT), even surpassing GPT-4o's 78%, and it's a reasoning-specialist model whose chain-of-thought pairs well with the plan-drafting stage. To use it, just change the `model`, `plan_model`, and `code_model` fields to the model name as loaded in LM Studio.
