2. Variables:

3. Constraints:

    - Constraint 2 (Each student works exactly two shifts): `ForAll([student], Sum([If(schedule[d * 2 + s] == student, 1, 0) for d in range(5) for s in range(2)]) == 2)`

    - Constraint 4 (No student works both shifts of any day): Implicitly enforced by Constraint 3.
    - Constraint 5 (Louise works two consecutive second shifts): `Or([And(schedule[d * 2 + 1] == 4, schedule[(d + 1) * 2 + 1] == 4) for d in range(4)])`
    - Constraint 6 (Grecia works two non-consecutive first shifts): `Or([And(schedule[d1 * 2 + 0] == 0, schedule[d2 * 2 + 0] == 0) for d1 in range(5) for d2 in range(5) if (d1 != d2 and abs(d1 - d2) != 1)])`
    - Constraint 7 (Katya works on Tuesday and Friday): `Or(schedule[1 * 2 + 0] == 3, schedule[1 * 2 + 1] == 3)`, `Or(schedule[4 * 2 + 0] == 3, schedule[4 * 2 + 1] == 3)`
    - Constraint 8 (Hakeem and Joe work on the same day at least once): `Or([Or(And(schedule[d * 2 + 0] == 1, schedule[d * 2 + 1] == 2), And(schedule[d * 2 + 0] == 2, schedule[d * 2 + 1] == 1)) for d in range(5)])`

    - Constraint 10 (Grecia and Joe work on the same day at least once): `Or([Or(And(schedule[d * 2 + 0] == 0, schedule[d * 2 + 1] == 2), And(schedule[d * 2 + 0] == 2, schedule[d * 2 + 1] == 0)) for d in range(5)])`
