1. **Analyze the Problem:**
    - 8 students, 6 slots (3 days x 2 slots/day).
    - 2 reports/day (1 morning, 1 afternoon).
    - Constraints on scheduling.
    - Find a valid schedule among options.

2. **Entities (Simplified):**
    - Students: George (G), Helen (H), Irving (I), Kyle (K), Lenore (L), Nina (N), Olivia (O), Robert (R).
    - Days: Monday (M), Tuesday (T), Wednesday (W).
    - Slots: MxM, MxA, TxM, TxA, WxM, WxA (Morning/Afternoon).

3. **Strategy:** Eliminate choices that violate constraints.

4. **Apply Constraints to Answer Choices:**

    - **Constraint 1: George only on Tuesday:**
        - Eliminates A, B, D, E.

    - **Constraint 2: No Olivia/Robert in afternoon:**
        - A violates this (Robert Mon afternoon).
        - D violates this (Olivia Wed morning, assuming morning is considered before afternoon, and only two reports per day).
        - E violates this (Nina Tues afternoon). Already eliminated.

    - **Constraint 3: Nina triggers Helen & Irving next day (unless Wednesday):**
        - B: Nina Wed, no violation.
        - D: Nina Mon, requires Helen & Irving Tues. Violated (Robert Tues morning, Irving Tues afternoon). Already eliminated.
        - E: Nina Tues, requires Helen & Irving Wed.  Already eliminated.

5. **Remaining Choice:**
    - Only C remains.

6. **Verify Choice C:**
    - C: M-L/H, T-G/K, W-R/I
    - All constraints satisfied.

7. **Conclusion:**
    - C is the only possible schedule.
