    - Constraint 2 (Distinctness): `Distinct([assignment[0], assignment[1], assignment[2]])`. Ensures each ambassador is assigned to at most one country.

Either Kayne or Novetzke is assigned, but not both.

    - Constraint 5 (Ong to Venezuela implies not Kayne to Yemen): `Implies(assignment[0] == 4, assignment[1] != 1)`. If Ong is assigned to Venezuela, Kayne is not assigned to Yemen.
    - Constraint 6 (Landon implies Zambia): `Implies(Or(assignment[0] == 2, assignment[1] == 2), False)`. If Landon is assigned, it must be to Zambia (and not to Venezuela or Yemen).
