    - Witnesses: Franco (F), Garcia (G), Hong (H), Iturbe (I), Jackson (J)
    - Days: Monday (M), Tuesday (T), Wednesday (W)

2. Variables:

    - Witness IDs: F=0, G=1, H=2, I=3, J=4
    - Day IDs: M=0, T=1, W=2

3. Constraints:

    - Constraint 2 (One day per witness): `Distinct([schedule[0], schedule[1], schedule[2], schedule[3], schedule[4]])` - Each witness testifies on a different day.
    - Constraint 3 (Franco != Garcia): `schedule[0] != schedule[1]`
    - Constraint 4 (Iturbe on Wednesday): `schedule[3] == 2`
    - Constraint 5 (Two on Tuesday): `Sum([If(schedule[w] == 1, 1, 0) for w in range(5)]) == 2`
    - Constraint 6 (Hong not on Monday): `schedule[2] != 0`
    - Constraint 7 (At least one on Monday): `Sum([If(schedule[w] == 0, 1, 0) for w in range(5)]) >= 1`
