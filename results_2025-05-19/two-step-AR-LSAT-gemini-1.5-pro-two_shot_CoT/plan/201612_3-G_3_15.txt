1. Entities:
    - Students: Franz (F=0), Greene (G=1), Hidalgo (H=2), Isaacs (I=3)
    - Paintings: Oil (O=0), Watercolor (W=1)
    - Walls: 1 (0), 2 (1), 3 (2), 4 (3)
    - Positions: Upper (U=0), Lower (L=1)

2. Variables:
    - `student_wall_position_painting = Array('student_wall_position_painting', IntSort(), IntSort(), IntSort(), IntSort())` - This array maps a student, wall, position, and painting type to a boolean value (0 or 1).  For example, `student_wall_position_painting[0][1][0][1] == 1` represents Franz (0) having a watercolor (1) displayed on wall 2 (1) in the upper position (0).

3. Constraints:
    - Constraint 1 (Each student displays exactly two paintings, one oil and one watercolor):
        `ForAll([s], And(Sum([If(student_wall_position_painting[s][w][p][0] == 1, 1, 0) for w in range(4) for p in range(2)]) == 1, Sum([If(student_wall_position_painting[s][w][p][1] == 1, 1, 0) for w in range(4) for p in range(2)]) == 1))`
        where `s = Int('s')`, `w = Int('w')`, `p = Int('p')`

    - Constraint 2 (Exactly two paintings are displayed on each wall, one upper and one lower):
        `ForAll([w], And(Sum([If(student_wall_position_painting[s][w][0][t] == 1, 1, 0) for s in range(4) for t in range(2)]) == 1, Sum([If(student_wall_position_painting[s][w][1][t] == 1, 1, 0) for s in range(4) for t in range(2)]) == 1))`
        where `s = Int('s')`, `w = Int('w')`, `t = Int('t')`

    - Constraint 3 (No wall has only watercolors):
        `ForAll([w], Or([student_wall_position_painting[s][w][p][0] == 1 for s in range(4) for p in range(2)]))`
        where `s = Int('s')`, `w = Int('w')`, `p = Int('p')`

    - Constraint 4 (No wall has the work of only one student):
        `ForAll([w], Exists([s1, s2], And(s1 != s2, Or([student_wall_position_painting[s1][w][p][t] == 1 for p in range(2) for t in range(2)]), Or([student_wall_position_painting[s2][w][p][t] == 1 for p in range(2) for t in range(2)]))))`
        where `s1 = Int('s1')`, `s2 = Int('s2')`, `w = Int('w')`, `p = Int('p')`, `t = Int('t')`

    - Constraint 5 (No wall has both a painting by Franz and a painting by Isaacs):
        `ForAll([w], Not(And(Or([student_wall_position_painting[0][w][p][t] == 1 for p in range(2) for t in range(2)]), Or([student_wall_position_painting[3][w][p][t] == 1 for p in range(2) for t in range(2)]))))`
        where `w = Int('w')`, `p = Int('p')`, `t = Int('t')`

    - Constraint 6 (Greene's watercolor is displayed in the upper position of the wall on which Franz's oil is displayed):
        `ForAll([w], Implies(Or([student_wall_position_painting[0][w][p][0] == 1 for p in range(2)]), student_wall_position_painting[1][w][0][1] == 1))`
        where `w = Int('w')`, `p = Int('p')`


    - Constraint 7 (Isaacs's oil is displayed in the lower position of wall 4):
        `student_wall_position_painting[3][3][1][0] == 1`

    - Constraint 8 (Greene's oil is displayed on the same wall as Franz's watercolor):
        `ForAll([w], Implies(Or([student_wall_position_painting[0][w][p][1] == 1 for p in range(2)]), Or([student_wall_position_painting[1][w][p][0] == 1 for p in range(2)])))`
        where `w = Int('w')`, `p = Int('p')`

4. Answering the Question:
    - Create a base Z3 solver with all general constraints (1-8).
    - For each answer choice:
        - Push the solver state.
        - Add the constraints corresponding to the negation of the answer choice.
        - Check `solver.check()`. If unsat, the original answer choice must be true.
        - Pop the solver state.
