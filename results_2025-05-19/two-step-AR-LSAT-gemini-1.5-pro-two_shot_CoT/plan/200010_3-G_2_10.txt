1. Entities:
- Works: French Novels (FN0, FN1, FN2), Russian Novels (RN0, RN1, RN2), French Plays (FP0, FP1), Russian Play (RP0).
- Total Works: 9
- Selected Works: At least 5, at most 6

2. Variables:
- `selected_works = Array('selected_works', IntSort(), BoolSort())`  Represents whether each work is selected. Indices 0-2: French Novels, 3-5: Russian Novels, 6-7: French Plays, 8: Russian Play.

3. Constraints:
- Constraint 1 (Total Works Selected): `5 <= Sum([If(selected_works[i], 1, 0) for i in range(9)]) <= 6`
- Constraint 2 (French Works Limit): `Sum([If(selected_works[i], 1, 0) for i in range(0,3)] + [If(selected_works[i], 1, 0) for i in range(6,8)]) <= 4`
- Constraint 3 (Novels Selected): `3 <= Sum([If(selected_works[i], 1, 0) for i in range(6)]) <= 4`
- Constraint 4 (French Novels vs Russian Novels): `Sum([If(selected_works[i], 1, 0) for i in range(3)]) >= Sum([If(selected_works[i], 1, 0) for i in range(3, 6)])`
- Constraint 5 (French Plays and Russian Play): `Implies(And(selected_works[6], selected_works[7]), Not(selected_works[8]))`

4. Answering the Question:
- Create a base Z3 solver with constraints 1-5.
- For each answer choice:
    - Push the solver state.
    - Add temporary constraints based on the answer choice's *negation*:
        - Choice A (one French novel and one French play): `Not(And(Or(selected_works[0], selected_works[1], selected_works[2]), Or(selected_works[6], selected_works[7])))`
        - Choice B (one French novel and one Russian play): `Not(And(Or(selected_works[0], selected_works[1], selected_works[2]), selected_works[8]))`
        - Choice C (one Russian novel and one French play): `Not(And(Or(selected_works[3], selected_works[4], selected_works[5]), Or(selected_works[6], selected_works[7])))`
        - Choice D (two French novels): `Sum([If(selected_works[i], 1, 0) for i in range(3)]) < 2`
        - Choice E (two Russian novels): `Sum([If(selected_works[i], 1, 0) for i in range(3, 6)]) < 2`
    - Check `solver.check()`. If unsat, this choice is a *necessary* condition.
    - Pop the solver state.
- The answer choice that results in 'unsat' is the correct answer.
