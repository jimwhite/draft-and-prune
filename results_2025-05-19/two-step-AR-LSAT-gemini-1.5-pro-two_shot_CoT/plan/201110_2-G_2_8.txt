3. Constraints:

    - Constraint 2 (Distinctness): `Distinct([assignment[0], assignment[1], assignment[2]])` - Each ambassador is assigned to exactly one country.

    - Constraint 5 (Ong to Venezuela implies Kayne not to Yemen): `Implies(assignment[0] == 4, assignment[1] != 1)` - If Ong is assigned to Venezuela, Kayne is not assigned to Yemen.
    - Constraint 6 (Landon to Zambia): `Implies(Or(assignment[0] == 2, assignment[1] == 2, assignment[2] == 2), assignment[2] == 2)` - If Landon is assigned, it's to Zambia.
