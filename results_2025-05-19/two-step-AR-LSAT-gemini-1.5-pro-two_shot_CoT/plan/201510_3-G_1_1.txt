1. Entities:
    - Accomplices: Peters (P=0), Quinn (Q=1), Rovero (R=2), Stanton (S=3), Tao (T=4), Villas (V=5), White (W=6)
    - Recruitment Order: Slots 0 through 6 (representing 1st to 7th recruitment).

2. Constraints:
    - Peters recruited fourth: P is at index 3.
    - Villas immediately before White: V is at index i, W is at index i+1.
    - Quinn recruited earlier than Rovero: Q is at index i, R is at index j, i < j.
    - Stanton not immediately before/after Tao: S is at index i, T is not at index i-1 or i+1.

3. Check each option:
    - Option A: Q, T, S, P, V, W, R
        - Satisfies P at 3, V before W, Q before R, S not adjacent to T.
    - Option B: Q, W, R, P, S, V, T
        - Violates V before W.
    - Option C: V, W, Q, S, P, T, R
        - Satisfies P at 3, V before W, Q before R, S not adjacent to T.
    - Option D: V, W, S, P, Q, T, R
        - Violates S not adjacent to T.
    - Option E: V, W, S, P, R, T, Q
        - Violates Q before R.

4. Possible correct answers: A and C.
