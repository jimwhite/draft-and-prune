1. Entities:
    - Botanists: F, G, H
    - Chemists: K, L, M
    - Zoologists: P, Q, R

2. Variables:
    - `on_panel = Array('on_panel', IntSort(), BoolSort())`  Represents whether a scientist (indexed 0-8, where 0-2 are botanists, 3-5 are chemists, and 6-8 are zoologists) is on the panel.

3. Constraints:
    - Constraint 1 (Panel Size): `Sum([If(on_panel[i], 1, 0) for i in range(9)]) == 5`
    - Constraint 2 (At least one of each type):
        - `Or(on_panel[0], on_panel[1], on_panel[2])`
        - `Or(on_panel[3], on_panel[4], on_panel[5])`
        - `Or(on_panel[6], on_panel[7], on_panel[8])`
    - Constraint 3 (More than one botanist implies at most one zoologist):
        - `Implies(Sum([If(on_panel[i], 1, 0) for i in range(3)]) > 1, Sum([If(on_panel[i], 1, 0) for i in range(6,9)]) <= 1)`
    - Constraint 4 (F and K mutual exclusion): `Not(And(on_panel[0], on_panel[3]))`
    - Constraint 5 (K and M mutual exclusion): `Not(And(on_panel[3], on_panel[5]))`
    - Constraint 6 (M implies P and R): `Implies(on_panel[5], And(on_panel[6], on_panel[8]))`
    - Constraint 7 (G and H are selected): `And(on_panel[1], on_panel[2])`

4. Answering the Question:
    - Create a base Z3 solver with constraints 1-7.
    - For each answer choice:
        - Push the solver state.
        - Add the negation of the constraint corresponding to the answer choice:
            - Choice A (F or K): `Not(Or(on_panel[0], on_panel[3]))`
            - Choice B (F or M): `Not(Or(on_panel[0], on_panel[5]))`
            - Choice C (K or M): `Not(Or(on_panel[3], on_panel[5]))`
            - Choice D (M or Q): `Not(Or(on_panel[5], on_panel[7]))`
            - Choice E (P or Q): `Not(Or(on_panel[6], on_panel[7]))`
        - Check `solver.check()`. If unsat, this choice *must* be true. Store the letter.
        - Pop the solver state.
    - The correct answer is the choice that resulted in 'unsat'.
