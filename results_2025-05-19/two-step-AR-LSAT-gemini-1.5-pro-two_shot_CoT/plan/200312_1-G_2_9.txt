- Constraint 5 (Textiles next to Produce): `Or([And(bay_cargo[i] == 5, bay_cargo[i+1] == 4) for i in range(1, 6)])`
- Constraint 6 (Produce next to Textiles): `Or([And(bay_cargo[i] == 4, bay_cargo[i+1] == 5) for i in range(1, 6)])`


4. Answering the Question:
