1. Entities:
    - Days: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7
    - Kitten Breeds: Himalayan (H=0), Manx (M=1), Siamese (S=2)
    - Puppy Breeds: Greyhound (G=0), Newfoundland (N=1), Rottweiler (R=2)

2. Variables:
    - `kitten_breed = Array('kitten_breed', IntSort(), IntSort())` # Maps day (1-7) to kitten breed (0-2)
    - `puppy_breed = Array('puppy_breed', IntSort(), IntSort())` # Maps day (1-7) to puppy breed (0-2)

3. Constraints:
    - Constraint 1 (Kitten and Puppy Breed Domains): `ForAll(d, And(And(kitten_breed[d] >= 0, kitten_breed[d] <= 2), And(puppy_breed[d] >= 0, puppy_breed[d] <= 2)))` where `d` ranges from 1 to 7.
    - Constraint 2 (One Kitten and Puppy per Day): `ForAll(d, Distinct(kitten_breed[d], puppy_breed[d]))` where `d` ranges from 1 to 7.
    - Constraint 3 (No Consecutive Breeds): `ForAll(d, And(Distinct(kitten_breed[d], kitten_breed[d+1]), Distinct(puppy_breed[d], puppy_breed[d+1])))` where `d` ranges from 1 to 6.
    - Constraint 4 (Greyhounds on Day 1): `puppy_breed[1] == G`
    - Constraint 5 (No Day 1 Breed on Day 7): `And(Distinct(kitten_breed[1], kitten_breed[7]), Distinct(puppy_breed[1], puppy_breed[7]))`
    - Constraint 6 (Himalayans on 3 Days, Not Day 1): `kitten_breed[1] != H`, `Sum([If(kitten_breed[d] == H, 1, 0) for d in range(1, 8)]) == 3`
    - Constraint 7 (No Rottweilers on Day 7 or with Himalayans): `puppy_breed[7] != R`, `ForAll(d, Implies(kitten_breed[d] == H, puppy_breed[d] != R))` where `d` ranges from 1 to 7.

            - C: `And(puppy_breed[4] == R, kitten_breed[4] == H)`
            - D: `And(puppy_breed[5] == R, kitten_breed[5] == M)`
            - E: `And(puppy_breed[6] == N, kitten_breed[6] == M)`
