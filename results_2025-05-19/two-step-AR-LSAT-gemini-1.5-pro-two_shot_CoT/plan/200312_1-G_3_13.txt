1. Entities:
    - Cookies: Oatmeal (O), Peanut Butter (P), Sugar (S)
    - Days: Monday (0), Tuesday (1), Wednesday (2), Thursday (3), Friday (4)
    - Batches: 1st (0), 2nd (1), 3rd (2)

2. Variables:
    - `schedule = Array('schedule', IntSort(), IntSort(), IntSort())`  # schedule[cookie][batch] = day

3. Constraints:
    - Constraint 1 (Domain): `ForAll([c, b], And(schedule[c][b] >= 0, schedule[c][b] <= 4))`
    - Constraint 2 (No two batches of the same kind on the same day): `ForAll([c, b1, b2], Implies(And(b1 != b2, b1 < 3, b2 < 3), schedule[c][b1] != schedule[c][b2]))`
    - Constraint 3 (At least one batch on Monday): `Or([Or(schedule[c][b] == 0) for c in range(3) for b in range(3)])`
    - Constraint 4 (Oatmeal 2nd batch and Peanut Butter 1st batch on same day): `schedule[0][1] == schedule[1][0]`
    - Constraint 5 (Sugar 2nd batch on Thursday): `schedule[2][1] == 3`
    - Constraint 6 (Three batches of each cookie): `ForAll([c], Distinct([schedule[c][b] for b in range(3)]))`
