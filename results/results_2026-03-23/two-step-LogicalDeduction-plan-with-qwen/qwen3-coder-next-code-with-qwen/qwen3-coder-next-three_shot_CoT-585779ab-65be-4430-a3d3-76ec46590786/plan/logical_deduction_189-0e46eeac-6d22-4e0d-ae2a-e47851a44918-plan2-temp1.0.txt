   * Add an `AllDifferentConstraint` to ensure each book occupies a unique position.

   * "The red book is the leftmost" → `red == 1`.  
   * "The gray book is the third from the right" → Since rightmost is 7, third from right = position 5 (7−2) → `gray == 5`.  
   * "The orange book is the rightmost" → `orange == 7`.

5. **Solve the Constraint Satisfaction Problem:**  
   * Call `getSolutions()` to find all valid assignments of positions to books.  
   * Extract the solution (there should be exactly one, given the constraints fully determine most positions and leave only two books ambiguous—green/blue—but their relative order is fixed by the constraint).
