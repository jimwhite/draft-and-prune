3.  **Add All-Different Constraint:**

    * "The peaches are more expensive than the plums" → `plums < peaches`.

            - 1st most expensive → rank 7

            - **3rd most expensive → rank 5**
    * "The loquats are the third-cheapest" → `loquats == 3` (since "k-th cheapest" = rank k: cheapest=1, 2nd-cheapest=2, **third-cheapest=3**).
