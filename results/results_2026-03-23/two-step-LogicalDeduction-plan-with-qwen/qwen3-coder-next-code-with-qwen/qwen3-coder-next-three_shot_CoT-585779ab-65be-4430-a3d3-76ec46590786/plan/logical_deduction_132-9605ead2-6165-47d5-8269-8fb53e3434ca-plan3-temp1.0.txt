     - "The bus is the newest" → `bus == 7`

     - "The motorcyle is the fourth-newest" → fourth-newest = position **4** → `motorcyle == 4`  
     - "The convertible is older than the tractor" → `convertible < tractor`

   * Call `getSolutions()` to enumerate all solutions satisfying the constraints.  
   * Extract the (unique) solution dictionary mapping each vehicle to its age rank.
