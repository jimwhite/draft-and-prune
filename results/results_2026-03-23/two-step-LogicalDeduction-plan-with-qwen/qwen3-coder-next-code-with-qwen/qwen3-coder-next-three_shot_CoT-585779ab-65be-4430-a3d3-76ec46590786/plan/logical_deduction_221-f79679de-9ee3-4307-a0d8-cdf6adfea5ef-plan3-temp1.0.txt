2. **Define Variables and Domain:**  
   * Define the variables as the seven fruits: `"oranges"`, `"loquats"`, `"apples"`, `"kiwis"`, `"mangoes"`, `"plums"`, and `"peaches"`.  
   * Define the domain as integers from 1 to 7, where **1 represents the cheapest** and **7 represents the most expensive**.  
   * Add all variables with their domain to the `Problem` instance.

3. **Formulate and Add Constraints:**  
   * Add an `AllDifferentConstraint` to enforce uniqueness of price rankings.  
   * Translate each statement into a constraint:  
     - "The apples are the second-cheapest" → `apples == 2`  
     - "The peaches are the cheapest" → `peaches == 1`  
     - "The plums are the fourth-most expensive" → `plums == 4` (since most expensive is 7, fourth-most expensive = 7 - 3 = 4)  
     - "The loquats are the third-cheapest" → `loquats == 3`  
     - "The kiwis are more expensive than the mangoes" → `kiwis > mangoes`  
     - "The oranges are less expensive than the mangoes" → `oranges < mangoes`

4. **Solve for the Arrangement:**  
   * Call `getSolutions()` to find all valid assignments.  
   * Since the constraints uniquely determine the order, extract the single solution from the list.
