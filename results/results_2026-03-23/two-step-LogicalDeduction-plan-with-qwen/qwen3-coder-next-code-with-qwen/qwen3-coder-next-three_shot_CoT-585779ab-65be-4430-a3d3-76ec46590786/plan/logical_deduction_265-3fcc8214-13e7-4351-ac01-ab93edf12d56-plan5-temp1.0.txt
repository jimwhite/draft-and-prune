    * Identify the variables: the three books — `"brown"`, `"yellow"`, and `"orange"`.

    * Add these variables with their domain (1–3) to the problem.

3.  **Add AllDifferent Constraint:**  
    * Enforce that each book occupies a distinct position using `AllDifferentConstraint`.

    * "The yellow book is the leftmost" → `yellow == 1`.  
    * "The orange book is to the right of the brown book" → `brown < orange`.

5.  **Solve the Problem:**  
    * Call `getSolutions()` to find all valid assignments.  
    * Extract the unique solution (a dictionary mapping book names to positions).
