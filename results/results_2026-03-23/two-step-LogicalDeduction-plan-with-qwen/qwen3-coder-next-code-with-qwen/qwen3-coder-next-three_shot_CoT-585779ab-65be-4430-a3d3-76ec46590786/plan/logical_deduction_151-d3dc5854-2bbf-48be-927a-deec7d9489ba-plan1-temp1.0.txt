3. **Formulate and Add Constraints:**  
   * Add an `AllDifferentConstraint` to enforce unique price ranks for each fruit.

     - "The pears are less expensive than the cantaloupes" → `pears < cantaloupes` (i.e., pears cheaper → smaller rank).  
     - "The peaches are the second-most expensive" → `peaches == 6` (since most expensive = 7, second-most = 6).  
     - "The mangoes are the cheapest" → `mangoes == 1`.  
     - "The kiwis are the third-cheapest" → `kiwis == 3` (cheapest = 1, second-cheapest = 2, third-cheapest = 3).

   * Call `getSolutions()` to compute all valid assignments. Given consistency and uniqueness, there should be exactly one solution.  
   * Retrieve the first (and only) solution dictionary mapping fruit names to their ranks.
