3. **Add Constraints from Statements:**  
   * Add an `AllDifferentConstraint` to enforce uniqueness of positions.  
   * Translate each statement into a constraint:  
     - "The purple book is the fourth from the left" → `purple == 4`  
     - "The brown book is the third from the left" → `brown == 3`  
     - "The green book is to the left of the blue book" → `green < blue`  
     - "The red book is the leftmost" → `red == 1`  
     - "The gray book is the third from the right" → Since there are 7 positions, third from the right = position `7 - 2 = 5` → `gray == 5`  
     - "The orange book is the rightmost" → `orange == 7`

4. **Solve the Constraint Satisfaction Problem:**  
   * Call `getSolutions()` to find all valid assignments.  
   * Extract the unique solution (should be a single dictionary mapping book names to positions).
