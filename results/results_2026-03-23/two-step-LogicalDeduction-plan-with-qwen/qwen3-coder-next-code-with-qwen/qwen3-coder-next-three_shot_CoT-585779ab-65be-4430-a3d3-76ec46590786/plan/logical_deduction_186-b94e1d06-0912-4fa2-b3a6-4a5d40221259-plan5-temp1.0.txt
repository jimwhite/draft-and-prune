2.  **Define Variables and Domain:**
    * Define the seven fruit variables: `"kiwis"`, `"plums"`, `"mangoes"`, `"watermelons"`, `"pears"`, `"peaches"`, `"oranges"`.
    * Assign integer ranks to represent price order, where **1 = cheapest** and **7 = most expensive**.
    * Add all seven variables to the problem with domain `{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7}`.

3.  **Add AllDifferentConstraint:**
    * Add an `AllDifferentConstraint` to enforce that each fruit has a unique price rank.

4.  **Translate Given Statements into Constraints:**
    * "The pears are the third-cheapest" → `pears == 3`.
    * "The kiwis are the second-most expensive" → `kiwis == 6` (since most expensive is 7, second-most is 6).
    * "The pears are more expensive than the plums" → `plums < pears` (i.e., `plums < 3`).
    * "The oranges are less expensive than the kiwis" → `oranges > kiwis` (i.e., `oranges > 6` ⇒ `oranges == 7`).
    * "The mangoes are the third-most expensive" → `mangoes == 5` (7 - 3 + 1 = 5).
    * "The peaches are the second-cheapest" → `peaches == 2`.

5.  **Solve the Problem:**
    * Call `getSolutions()` to find all valid assignments satisfying all constraints.
    * Extract the unique solution (since constraints fully determine positions).

6.  **Identify the Cheapest Fruit:**
    * The cheapest fruit has rank `1`.
    * Iterate through the solution dictionary to find which fruit maps to rank `1`.
