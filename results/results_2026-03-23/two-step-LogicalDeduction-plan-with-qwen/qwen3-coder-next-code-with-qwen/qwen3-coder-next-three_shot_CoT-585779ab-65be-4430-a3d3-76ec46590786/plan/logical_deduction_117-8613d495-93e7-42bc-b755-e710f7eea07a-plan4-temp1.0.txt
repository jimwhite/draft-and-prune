   * Add an `AllDifferentConstraint` to ensure each golfer has a unique finishing position.

     - `"Amy finished above Rob"` → `amy < rob` (i.e., `3 < rob` ⇒ `rob > 3`)  

4. **Solve the Constraint Satisfaction Problem:**  
   * Call `getSolutions()` to compute all valid position assignments.  
   * Extract the unique solution (as the constraints uniquely determine the ranking).
