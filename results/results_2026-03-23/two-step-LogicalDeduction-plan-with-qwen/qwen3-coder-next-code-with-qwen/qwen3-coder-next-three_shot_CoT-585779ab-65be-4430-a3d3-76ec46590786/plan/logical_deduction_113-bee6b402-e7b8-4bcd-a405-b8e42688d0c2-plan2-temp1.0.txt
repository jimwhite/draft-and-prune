    * Define a domain of integers from 1 to 7, where **1 represents the cheapest** and **7 represents the most expensive**.
    * Add all variables with domain {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7} to the `Problem` instance.

3.  **Formulate and Add Constraints:**
    * Add an `AllDifferentConstraint` to ensure each fruit has a unique price rank.
    * Convert each statement into a constraint:
        * "The watermelons are the cheapest" → `watermelons == 1`.
        * "The peaches are more expensive than the mangoes" → `peaches > mangoes`.
        * "The cantaloupes are the second-most expensive" → `cantaloupes == 6` (since 7 is most expensive).
        * "The oranges are more expensive than the cantaloupes" → `oranges > cantaloupes`.
        * "The peaches are less expensive than the plums" → `peaches < plums`.
        * "The kiwis are the third-cheapest" → `kiwis == 3`.

4.  **Solve the Problem:**
    * Call `getSolutions()` to find all valid price-rank assignments.
    * Extract the unique solution (only one will satisfy all constraints).
