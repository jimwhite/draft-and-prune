3.  **Add All-Different Constraint:**
    * Add an `AllDifferentConstraint` to ensure no two books occupy the same position.

    * "The red book is the second from the right" → `red == 6` (since position 7 is rightmost, second from right is 6).
    * "The black book is the fourth from the left" → `black == 4`.
    * "The orange book is to the left of the white book" → `orange < white`.

5.  **Solve the Constraint Satisfaction Problem:**
    * Call `getSolutions()` to retrieve all valid assignments.
