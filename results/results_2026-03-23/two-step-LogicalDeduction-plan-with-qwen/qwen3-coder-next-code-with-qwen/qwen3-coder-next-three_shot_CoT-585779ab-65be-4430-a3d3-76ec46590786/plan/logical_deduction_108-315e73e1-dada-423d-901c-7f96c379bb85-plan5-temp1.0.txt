     - "The plums are the second-most expensive" → rank = 6 (`plums == 6`).  
     - "The loquats are the second-cheapest" → rank = 2 (`loquats == 2`).

   * Use `getSolutions()` to find all solutions satisfying the constraints.  
   * Extract the unique solution (problem implies consistency and uniqueness).

   * From the solution, find which fruit has value `5`.
