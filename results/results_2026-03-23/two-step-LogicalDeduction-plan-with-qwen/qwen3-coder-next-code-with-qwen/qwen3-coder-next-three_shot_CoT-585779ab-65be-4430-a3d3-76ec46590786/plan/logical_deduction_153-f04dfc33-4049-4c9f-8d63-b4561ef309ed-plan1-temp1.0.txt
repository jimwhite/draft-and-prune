    * Define the seven book variables: `"yellow"`, `"red"`, `"gray"`, `"blue"`, `"white"`, `"orange"`, `"purple"`.
    * Define the domain as integers from 1 to 7, where position 1 = leftmost and position 7 = rightmost.
    * Add all variables with this domain to the `Problem` instance.

3.  **Formulate and Add Constraints Based on Statements:**

        * "The white book is to the right of the orange book" → `orange < white`.
        * "The purple book is the third from the left" → position = 3.
        * "The yellow book is to the right of the purple book" → `purple < yellow`.
        * "The blue book is to the right of the red book" → `red < blue`.
        * "The blue book is to the left of the gray book" → `blue < gray`.

4.  **Solve the Constraint Satisfaction Problem:**
    * Call `getSolutions()` to find all valid assignments of positions to books.
    * Extract the solution (only one expected, as implied by consistency).

5.  **Determine Which Book is Second from the Left (Position 2):**
