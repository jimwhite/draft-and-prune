3. **Formulate Constraints from Statements:**  
   * Add `AllDifferentConstraint()` to enforce uniqueness of positions.

     - "The cardinal is the fourth from the left" → `cardinal == 4`.  
     - "The falcon is to the left of the blue jay" → `falcon < blue_jay`.  
     - "The quail is to the left of the falcon" → `quail < falcon`.  
     - "The raven is the second from the left" → `raven == 2`.  
     - "The robin is to the left of the quail" → `robin < quail`.

4. **Solve the Problem:**  
   * Call `getSolutions()` to find all valid assignments.  
   * Extract the unique solution (since constraints fully determine ordering).
