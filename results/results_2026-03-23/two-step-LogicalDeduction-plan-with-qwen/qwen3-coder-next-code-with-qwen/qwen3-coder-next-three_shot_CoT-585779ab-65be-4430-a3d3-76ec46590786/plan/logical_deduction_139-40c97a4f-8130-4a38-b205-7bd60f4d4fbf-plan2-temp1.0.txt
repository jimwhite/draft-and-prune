   * Define the domain as integers from 1 to 7, where **1 = leftmost** and **7 = rightmost**.

        - "The blue jay is the fourth from the left" → `bluejay == 4`  
        - "The robin is the second from the left" → `robin == 2`  
        - "The quail is to the right of the crow" → `quail > crow`

4. **Solve the Constraint Satisfaction Problem:**  
   * Call `getSolutions()` on the problem instance to enumerate all valid assignments.  
   * Since the problem states it is logically consistent and uniquely determined, there should be exactly one solution. Extract that unique solution dictionary.
