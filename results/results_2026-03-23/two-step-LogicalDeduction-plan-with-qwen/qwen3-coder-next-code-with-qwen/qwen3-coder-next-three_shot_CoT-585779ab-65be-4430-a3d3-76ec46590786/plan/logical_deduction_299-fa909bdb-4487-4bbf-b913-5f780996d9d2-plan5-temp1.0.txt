        * "The red book is the second from the left" → `red == 2`.

    * Call `getSolutions()` to find all valid assignments. Since the constraints fully determine the ordering, there will be exactly one solution.  
    * Retrieve the sole solution from the returned list (e.g., `solutions[0]`).
