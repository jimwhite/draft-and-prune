    * Define the position domain as integers from 1 to 7, where **1 = leftmost** and **7 = rightmost**.
    * Add all seven bird variables with domain `{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7}` to the problem.

3.  **Add Constraints:**
    * Add an `AllDifferentConstraint` to enforce uniqueness of positions.

        * "The falcon is the second from the left" → `falcon == 2`.
        * "The crow is the rightmost" → `crow == 7`.
        * "The hummingbird is to the right of the blue jay" → `blue jay < hummingbird`.
        * "The hawk is to the right of the hummingbird" → `hummingbird < hawk`.
        * "The blue jay is to the right of the owl" → `owl < blue jay`.
        * "The raven is to the left of the falcon" → `raven < falcon` (and since `falcon == 2`, this implies `raven == 1`).

4.  **Solve the Problem:**
    * Call `getSolutions()` to find all valid assignments.
    * Extract the unique solution (since constraints fully determine positions).
