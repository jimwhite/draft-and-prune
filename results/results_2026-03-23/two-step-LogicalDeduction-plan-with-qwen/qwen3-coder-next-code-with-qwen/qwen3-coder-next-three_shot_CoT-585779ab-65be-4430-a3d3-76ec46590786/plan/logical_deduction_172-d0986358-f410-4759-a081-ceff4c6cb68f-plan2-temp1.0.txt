    * Identify the seven vehicles as variables: `"limousine"`, `"convertible"`, `"station_wagon"`, `"minivan"`, `"bus"`, `"tractor"`, and `"truck"`.
    * Define the domain as integers from 1 to 7, where **1 represents the oldest** and **7 represents the newest** vehicle.
    * Add all seven variables with this domain to the `Problem` instance.

3.  **Add AllDifferentConstraint:**
    * Add an `AllDifferentConstraint` to ensure each vehicle has a unique age rank.

4.  **Translate Each Statement into Formal Constraints:**
    * "The minivan is the oldest" → `minivan == 1`
    * "The truck is older than the station wagon" → `truck < station_wagon`
    * "The truck is newer than the limousine" → `truck > limousine`
    * "The bus is newer than the convertible" → `bus > convertible`
    * "The bus is older than the tractor" → `bus < tractor`
    * "The limousine is newer than the tractor" → `limousine > tractor`

5.  **Solve the Problem:**
    * Call `getSolutions()` to find all valid assignments of ranks satisfying the constraints.
    * Extract the unique solution (since the problem is deterministic) from the list of solutions.

6.  **Interpret "third-newest" Using the Ranking System:**
    * Since 7 is newest, 6 is second-newest, and **5 is third-newest**.
    * Therefore, identify the vehicle whose rank in the solution is exactly 5.
