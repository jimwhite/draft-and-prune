3. **Add Constraints:**  
   * Add an `AllDifferentConstraint` to ensure each bird occupies a unique position.  
   * Add the following constraints using `addConstraint` with lambda functions:

     - "The owl is the second from the left" → `lambda o: o == 2`  
     - "The robin is to the left of the hummingbird" → `lambda r, h: r < h`  
     - "The quail is to the right of the falcon" → `lambda q, f: q > f`  
     - "The robin is to the right of the owl" → `lambda r, o: r > o`  
     - "The blue jay is the third from the right" → Since positions are 1 (leftmost) to 7 (rightmost), third from the right is position **5** → `lambda bj: bj == 5`

4. **Solve the Problem:**  
   * Call `getSolutions()` to find all valid arrangements.  
   * Extract the unique solution (the list should contain exactly one dictionary mapping bird names to positions).
