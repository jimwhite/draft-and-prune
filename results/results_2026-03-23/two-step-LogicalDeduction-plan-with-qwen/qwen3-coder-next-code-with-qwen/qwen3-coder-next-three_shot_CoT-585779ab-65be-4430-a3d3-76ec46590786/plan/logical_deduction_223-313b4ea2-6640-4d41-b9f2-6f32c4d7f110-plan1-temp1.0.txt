    * Define the domain as integers from 1 to 7, where 1 = leftmost position and 7 = rightmost position.  
    * Add all seven variables with domain {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7} to the problem.

3.  **Add AllDifferent Constraint:**  
    * Add an `AllDifferentConstraint` to ensure each bird occupies a unique position.

4.  **Translate Statements into Constraints:**  
    * "The hummingbird is to the left of the quail" → `hummingbird < quail` (use a lambda constraint: `lambda h, q: h < q`).  
    * "The robin is to the left of the cardinal" → `robin < cardinal`.  
    * "The blue jay is the leftmost" → `blue_jay == 1`.  
    * "The cardinal is the fourth from the left" → `cardinal == 4`.  
    * "The raven is the third from the right" → in a 7-position line, third from the right = position 5 (since positions 5,6,7 are rightmost three), so `raven == 5`.  
    * "The owl is the third from the left" → `owl == 3`.

5.  **Solve the Constraint Satisfaction Problem:**  
    * Call `getSolutions()` to find all satisfying assignments.  
    * Since the problem is well-constrained, expect a unique solution; extract it from the list.

6.  **Identify the Leftmost Bird:**  
    * The question asks which bird is leftmost (i.e., position 1).  
    * From the solved assignment, find which bird variable has value 1.
