   * Define a numerical domain from 1 to 5, where **1 represents the cheapest** and **5 represents the most expensive**.  
   * Add all fruit variables to the problem with domain `{1, 2, 3, 4, 5}`.  

3. **Formulate and Add Constraints:**  
   * Add an `AllDifferentConstraint` to ensure each fruit has a unique price rank.  
   * Translate the statements into constraints:  
     - "The kiwis are less expensive than the mangoes" → `kiwi < mango`  
     - "The peaches are less expensive than the oranges" → `peach < orange`  
     - "The oranges are the second-most expensive" → Since most expensive = 5, second-most expensive = 4 ⇒ `orange == 4`  
     - "The mangoes are the second-cheapest" → Cheapest = 1, so second-cheapest = 2 ⇒ `mango == 2`  

4. **Solve the Problem:**  
   * Call `getSolutions()` to find all valid assignments of price ranks satisfying the constraints.  
   * Extract the unique solution (the problem is deterministic).
