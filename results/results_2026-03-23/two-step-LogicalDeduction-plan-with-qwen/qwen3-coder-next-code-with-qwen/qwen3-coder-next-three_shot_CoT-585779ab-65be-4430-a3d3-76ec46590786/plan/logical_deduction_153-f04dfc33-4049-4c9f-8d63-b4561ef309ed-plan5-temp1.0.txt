    * Add all seven variables to the problem with their domain (1–7).

3.  **Formulate and Add Constraints:**

        - "The red book is the third from the right" → red position = 5 (since 7−3+1=5).

        - "The purple book is the third from the left" → purple position = 3.
        - "The yellow book is to the right of the purple book" → `yellow > purple` (i.e., yellow position > 3).
        - "The blue book is to the right of the red book" → `blue > red` (i.e., blue position > 5).
        - "The blue book is to the left of the gray book" → `blue < gray`.

4.  **Solve for the Arrangement:**
    * Use `getSolutions()` to compute all valid assignments.
    * Extract the unique solution (or one solution, since the problem is constrained enough to yield uniqueness).
