    * Import the `Problem` and `AllDifferentConstraint` classes from the `constraint` library.

2.  **Define Variables and Domain:**
    * The variables are the seven fruits: `"kiwis"`, `"cantaloupes"`, `"oranges"`, `"loquats"`, `"pears"`, `"watermelons"`, and `"peaches"`.
    * Define the domain as integers from 1 to 7, where **1 = cheapest** and **7 = most expensive**.
    * Add all seven fruit variables with domain {1,2,3,4,5,6,7} to the problem.

3.  **Formulate and Add Constraints:**
    * Add an `AllDifferentConstraint` to ensure each fruit has a unique price rank.
    * Translate each statement into a constraint:
        - "The loquats are less expensive than the peaches" → `loquats < peaches`
        - "The pears are the third-cheapest" → `pears == 3`
        - "The oranges are less expensive than the cantaloupes" → `oranges < cantaloupes`
        - "The loquats are more expensive than the watermelons" → `watermelons < loquats`
        - "The peaches are less expensive than the oranges" → `peaches < oranges`
        - "The kiwis are the second-most expensive" → `kiwis == 6` (since 7 is most expensive)

4.  **Solve the Problem:**
    * Call `getSolutions()` to compute all valid assignments of ranks to fruits.
    * Extract the unique solution (the problem is deterministic given the constraints).

5.  **Identify the Most Expensive Fruit:**
    * The most expensive fruit corresponds to rank **7**.
    * Create a mapping from choice letters ('A'–'G') to fruit names based on the choices.
    * In the solution dictionary, locate the fruit with value 7.
