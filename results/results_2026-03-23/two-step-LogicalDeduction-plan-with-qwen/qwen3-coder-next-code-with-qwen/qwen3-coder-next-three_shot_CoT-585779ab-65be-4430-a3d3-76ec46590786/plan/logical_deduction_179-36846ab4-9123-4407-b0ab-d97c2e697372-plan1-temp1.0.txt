    * Define the domain for positions as integers from 1 to 7, where **1 = leftmost** and **7 = rightmost**.  
    * Add all seven bird variables with domain {1,2,3,4,5,6,7} to the problem.

3.  **Formulate Constraints from Statements:**  
    * Add an `AllDifferentConstraint` to enforce uniqueness of positions.  
    * Convert each natural-language statement into a constraint:  
        - "The quail is to the left of the hummingbird" → `quail < hummingbird`  
        - "The raven is to the right of the hummingbird" → `hummingbird < raven`  
        - "The cardinal is the second from the left" → `cardinal == 2`  
        - "The owl is to the left of the cardinal" → `owl < cardinal` (i.e., `owl < 2`)  
        - "The raven is the third from the right" → Since rightmost is 7, third from right = position 5 → `raven == 5`  
        - "The falcon is the rightmost" → `falcon == 7`

4.  **Solve the Constraint Satisfaction Problem:**  
    * Call `getSolutions()` to find all satisfying assignments. Given the constraints, there should be a unique solution.
