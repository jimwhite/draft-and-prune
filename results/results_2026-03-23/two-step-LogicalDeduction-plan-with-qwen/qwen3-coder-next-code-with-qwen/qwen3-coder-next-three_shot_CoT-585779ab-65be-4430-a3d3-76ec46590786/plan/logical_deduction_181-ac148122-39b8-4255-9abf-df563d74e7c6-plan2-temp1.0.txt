3. **Formulate and Add Constraints:**

        - "The gray book is the fourth from the left" → `gray == 4`  
        - "The black book is the rightmost" → `black == 7`  
        - "The gray book is to the right of the red book" → `red < gray`

4. **Solve for Valid Arrangements:**  
   * Call `getSolutions()` to retrieve all solutions that satisfy the constraints.  
   * Extract the solution(s); due to the problem's constraints, there should be a unique valid assignment.
