2.  **Define Variables and Their Domain:**
    * Identify the five fruits as variables: `"cantaloupe"`, `"kiwi"`, `"orange"`, `"mango"`, and `"peach"`.
    * Define a domain of integers from 1 to 5, where **1 represents the least expensive (cheapest)** and **5 represents the most expensive**.
    * Add all fruit variables with this domain to the `Problem` instance.

3.  **Formulate and Add Constraints:**
    * Add an `AllDifferentConstraint` to ensure each fruit has a unique price rank.
    * Translate each statement into constraints using `lambda` functions:
        * "The kiwis are less expensive than the mangoes" → `kiwi < mango`
        * "The peaches are less expensive than the oranges" → `peach < orange`
        * "The oranges are the second-most expensive" → `orange == 4` (since most expensive = 5, second-most = 4)
        * "The mangoes are the second-cheapest" → `mango == 2` (since cheapest = 1, second-cheapest = 2)

4.  **Solve the Problem:**
    * Use `getSolutions()` to find all valid assignments of price ranks.
    * Extract the unique solution (since the problem is constrained enough to yield a single arrangement).

5.  **Determine the Correct Answer:**
