   * Import the `Problem` and `AllDifferentConstraint` classes from the `constraint` library.

2. **Define Variables and Domain:**  
   * Define the seven fruits as variables: `"mangoes"`, `"cantaloupes"`, `"apples"`, `"plums"`, `"loquats"`, `"watermelons"`, and `"peaches"`.  
   * Assign a numerical domain from 1 to 7, where **1 represents the cheapest** and **7 represents the most expensive**, as implied by "more expensive" and comparative phrases.  
   * Add all variables to the problem with this domain.

3. **Add AllDifferent Constraint:**  
   * Enforce that each fruit has a unique price rank using `AllDifferentConstraint`.

4. **Translate Each Statement into Constraints:**  
   * "The plums are more expensive than the mangoes" → `plums < mangoes` (since lower rank number = cheaper).  
   * "The watermelons are more expensive than the loquats" → `watermelons < loquats`.  
   * "The loquats are the second-cheapest" → `loquats == 2`.  
   * "The plums are less expensive than the peaches" → `plums > peaches`.  
   * "The mangoes are the fourth-most expensive" → `mangoes == 4`.  
   * "The peaches are less expensive than the cantaloupes" → `peaches > cantaloupes`.

5. **Solve the Problem:**  
   * Call `getSolutions()` to compute all valid assignments of ranks to fruits.  
   * Extract the unique solution (since the problem is fully constrained).

6. **Identify the Cheapest Fruit:**

   * Build a mapping from choice letters (`'A'` through `'G'`) to fruit names as per the choices:  
     - `'A'`: `"mangoes"`
