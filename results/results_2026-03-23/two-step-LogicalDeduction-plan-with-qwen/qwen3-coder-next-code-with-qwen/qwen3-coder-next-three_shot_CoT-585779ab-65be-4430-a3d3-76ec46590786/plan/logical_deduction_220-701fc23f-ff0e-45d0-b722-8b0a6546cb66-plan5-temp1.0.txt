    * Define the domain as integers from 1 to 7, where **1 = leftmost** and **7 = rightmost** position.
    * Add all variables with domain {1,2,3,4,5,6,7} to the `Problem` instance.

3.  **Formulate and Add Constraints:**
    * Add an `AllDifferentConstraint` to ensure each book occupies a unique position.

        * "The red book is to the right of the white book" → `red > white`
        * "The gray book is to the left of the orange book" → `gray < orange`
        * "The gray book is to the right of the blue book" → `gray > blue`
        * "The red book is the second from the right" → `red == 6` (since position 7 is rightmost)
        * "The black book is the fourth from the left" → `black == 4`
        * "The orange book is to the left of the white book" → `orange < white`

4.  **Solve the Problem:**
    * Call `getSolutions()` to obtain all valid assignments.
    * Extract the unique solution from the list of solutions (assume exactly one satisfies all constraints).
