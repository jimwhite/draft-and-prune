    * Define the domain as integers 1 through 5, where **1 represents the cheapest** and **5 represents the most expensive** fruit.
    * Add all variables with this domain to the `Problem` instance.

3.  **Translate Statements into Constraints:**
    * Add an `AllDifferentConstraint` to ensure each fruit has a unique price rank.

        * "The watermelons are more expensive than the cantaloupes" → `watermelons > cantaloupes`.
        * "The mangoes are less expensive than the pears" → `mangoes < pears`.
        * "The apples are the second-cheapest" → `apples == 2` (since 1 is cheapest, 2 is second-cheapest).
        * "The watermelons are less expensive than the mangoes" → `watermelons < mangoes`.

4.  **Solve the Problem:**
    * Call `getSolutions()` to find all valid assignments of ranks to fruits.
    * Extract the unique solution (only one should satisfy all constraints).
