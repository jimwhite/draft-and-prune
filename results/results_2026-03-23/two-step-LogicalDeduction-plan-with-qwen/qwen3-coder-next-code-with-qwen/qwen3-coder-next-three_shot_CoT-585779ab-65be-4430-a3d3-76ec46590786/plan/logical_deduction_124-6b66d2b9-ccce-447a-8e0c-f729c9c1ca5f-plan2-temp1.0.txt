   * Define the domain for positions as integers from 1 to 7, where **1 = leftmost** and **7 = rightmost**.  
   * Add all seven variables to the problem with the domain {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7}.

3. **Add All-Different Constraint:**  
   * Apply an `AllDifferentConstraint` to ensure each book occupies a unique position.

4. **Translate Each Statement into Constraints Using Lambda Functions:**  
   * "The red book is to the right of the yellow book" → `red > yellow`  
   * "The white book is the second from the right" → `white == 6` (since position 7 is rightmost, second-right = 6)  
   * "The red book is to the left of the green book" → `red < green`  
   * "The blue book is to the right of the black book" → `blue > black`  
   * "The black book is the third from the right" → `black == 5` (positions: 7=rightmost, 6=second-right, 5=third-right)  
   * "The orange book is the leftmost" → `orange == 1`

5. **Solve the Problem:**  
   * Call `getSolutions()` to enumerate all valid assignments.  
   * Extract the unique solution (since the constraints fully determine a single ordering).
