3.  **Add Constraints:**

        - "The white book is to the left of the yellow book" → `white < yellow`  
        - "The blue book is the second from the right" → `blue == 4` (since position 5 is rightmost, so second from right is 4)  
        - "The purple book is the second from the left" → `purple == 2`

4.  **Solve the Problem:**  
    * Use `getSolutions()` to compute all valid assignments.  
    * Extract the unique solution (as the problem is consistent and constrained enough to yield one).
