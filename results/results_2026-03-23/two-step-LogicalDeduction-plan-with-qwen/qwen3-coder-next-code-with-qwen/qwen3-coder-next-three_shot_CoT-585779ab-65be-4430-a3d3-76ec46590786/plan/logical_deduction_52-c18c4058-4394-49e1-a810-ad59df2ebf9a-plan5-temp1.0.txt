3.  **Formulate and Add Constraints:**  
    * Add `AllDifferentConstraint()` to ensure all birds occupy distinct positions.

        - "The cardinal is to the left of the hawk" → `cardinal < hawk`  
        - "The raven is the second from the right" → `raven == 4` (since positions are left-to-right: 1,2,3,4,5 → rightmost=5, second from right=4)  

4.  **Solve the Problem:**  
    * Call `getSolutions()` to compute all satisfying assignments.
