     - "The yellow book is to the right of the orange book" → `orange < yellow`  
     - "The blue book is to the right of the white book" → `white < blue`  

4. **Solve for the Arrangement:**  
   * Call `getSolutions()` to compute all valid solutions.  
   * Extract the unique solution (as the problem is constrained enough to have one solution).
