    * Set the domain as integers from 1 to 7, where **1 = leftmost position** and **7 = rightmost position**.
    * Add all seven variables with domain {1,2,3,4,5,6,7} to the problem.

3.  **Add All-Different Constraint:**
    * Enforce that all books occupy distinct positions by adding an `AllDifferentConstraint` over the seven book variables.

4.  **Translate Each Statement into Constraints:**
    * "The yellow book is the rightmost" → `yellow == 7`.
    * "The blue book is to the left of the orange book" → `blue < orange`.
    * "The green book is to the right of the white book" → `white < green`.
    * "The blue book is to the right of the green book" → `green < blue`.
    * "The black book is the fourth from the left" → `black == 4`.
    * "The brown book is the third from the right" → In a 7-position line, third from the right means position `7 - 3 + 1 = 5`, so `brown == 5`.

5.  **Solve the Problem:**
    * Call `getSolutions()` to find all valid assignments of positions satisfying the constraints.
    * Extract the unique solution (since the problem is deterministic and consistent).

6.  **Interpret the Question:**

    * Identify the book assigned to position 3 in the solution.
