3.  **Formulate and Add Constraints:**  
    * Add an `AllDifferentConstraint` to ensure all books occupy distinct positions.

        - "The yellow book is the rightmost" → `yellow == 7`  
        - "The blue book is to the left of the orange book" → `blue < orange`  
        - "The green book is to the right of the white book" → `white < green`  
        - "The blue book is to the right of the green book" → `green < blue`  
        - "The black book is the fourth from the left" → `black == 4`  
        - "The brown book is the third from the right" → since there are 7 positions, third from the right = position 5 (`brown == 5`)

4.  **Solve for the Arrangement:**  
    * Call `getSolutions()` to find all valid assignments. Given the constraints, there should be a unique solution.  
    * Retrieve the single solution (or first solution) from the list of solutions.
