    * Define the position domain as integers 1 through 5, where 1 = leftmost and 5 = rightmost.
    * Add all five variables with domain {1,2,3,4,5} to the `Problem` instance.

3.  **Formulate and Add Constraints:**
    * Add an `AllDifferentConstraint` to enforce that each bird occupies a unique position.
    * Translate the statements into constraints:
        * "The robin is to the right of the cardinal" → `robin > cardinal`
        * "The cardinal is to the right of the blue jay" → `cardinal > blue_jay`
        * "The blue jay is the second from the left" → `blue_jay == 2`
        * "The crow is the rightmost" → `crow == 5`

4.  **Solve for All Valid Arrangements:**
    * Call `getSolutions()` on the problem instance to retrieve all satisfying assignments.
    * Extract the unique solution (only one will exist due to problem constraints).
