1.  **Initialize the Constraint Satisfaction Problem:**  
    * Import `Problem` and `AllDifferentConstraint` from the `constraint` library.  
    * Create a new `Problem()` instance.

2.  **Define Variables and Domain:**  
    * Define the seven book variables: `"orange"`, `"red"`, `"green"`, `"white"`, `"purple"`, `"brown"`, `"yellow"`.  
    * Set the domain as integers 1 through 7, where **1 = leftmost** and **7 = rightmost**.  
    * Add all variables to the problem with this domain.

3.  **Add AllDifferentConstraint:**  
    * Add `AllDifferentConstraint()` to ensure each book occupies a unique position.

4.  **Translate Statements into Positional Constraints:**  
    * "The green book is to the left of the purple book" → `green < purple` (using lambda constraint).  
    * "The white book is to the right of the orange book" → `orange < white`.  
    * "The green book is to the right of the brown book" → `brown < green`.  
    * "The yellow book is to the right of the white book" → `white < yellow`.  
    * "The red book is to the right of the yellow book" → `yellow < red`.  
    * "The brown book is to the right of the red book" → `red < brown`.  

5.  **Solve the Problem:**  
    * Use `getSolutions()` to obtain all valid assignments.  
    * Extract the unique solution (since consistency is guaranteed and ordering constraints are tight).

6.  **Determine the Second-from-the-Left Book:**  
    * Identify the book whose position value is 2 in the solution.  
    * Create a mapping from choice letters (`'A'` to `'G'`) to book names based on the choices:  
        * `'A'`: `"orange"`, `'B'`: `"red"`, `'C'`: `"green"`, `'D'`: `"white"`, `'E'`: `"purple"`, `'F'`: `"brown"`, `'G'`: `"yellow"`.
