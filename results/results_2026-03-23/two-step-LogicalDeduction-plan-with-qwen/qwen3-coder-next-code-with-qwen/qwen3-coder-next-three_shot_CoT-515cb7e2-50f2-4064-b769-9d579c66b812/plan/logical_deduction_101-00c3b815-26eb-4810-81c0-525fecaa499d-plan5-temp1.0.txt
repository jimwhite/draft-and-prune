3.  **Add All-Different Constraint:**
    * Enforce that all fruits have distinct price rankings using `AllDifferentConstraint`.

4.  **Translate Relative Order Statements into Numeric Constraints:**
    * "The pears are more expensive than the oranges" → `pears > oranges`.
    * "The oranges are more expensive than the cantaloupes" → `oranges > cantaloupes`.
    * "The peaches are less expensive than the cantaloupes" → `peaches < cantaloupes`.
    * Combine to derive a chain: `peaches < cantaloupes < oranges < pears`.
    * "The apples are the third-cheapest" → `apples == 3`.
    * "The watermelons are the second-most expensive" → among 7 items, most expensive = 7, second-most expensive = 6 → `watermelons == 6`.
    * "The mangoes are the fourth-most expensive" → fourth-most expensive = position 4 (since 7−3=4) → `mangoes == 4`.

5.  **Solve the Constraint Problem:**
    * Use `getSolutions()` to find all valid assignments.
    * Retrieve the unique solution (or one solution, as uniqueness is implied).
