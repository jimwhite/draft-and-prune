3. Add **one-per-country** constraints: for each country i, exactly one candidate j has `assign[i][j] = True`.  
4. Add **one-per-candidate** constraints: for each candidate j, at most one country i has `assign[i][j] = True`.

7. Add **constraint C**: If Ong (index 4) is assigned to Venezuela (index 0), then Kayne (index 1) must *not* be assigned to Yemen (index 1): `assign[0][4] → ¬(assign[1][1])`.

9. Translate the five answer choices into pairs of candidate indices:  
   - Choice 0: Jaramillo (0) and Novetzke (3) unassigned

   - Choice 3: Kayne (1) and Novetzke (3) unassigned
