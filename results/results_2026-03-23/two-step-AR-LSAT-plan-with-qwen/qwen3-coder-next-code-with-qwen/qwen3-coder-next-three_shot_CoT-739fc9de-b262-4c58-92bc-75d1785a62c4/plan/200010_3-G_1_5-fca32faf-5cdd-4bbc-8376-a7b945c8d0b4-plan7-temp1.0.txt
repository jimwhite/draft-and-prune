4. Add **two-reports-per-day** constraint: for each day d, sum over i,s of `r[i][d][s]` = 2.  
5. Add **one-report-per-slot** constraint: for each day d and slot s, at most one student can occupy that slot (i.e., sum over i of `r[i][d][s]` ≤ 1; combined with previous, it becomes exactly one per slot).

    - Since exactly one report per slot and two per day, this means both slots on d+1 are taken by Helen and Irving in some order.
