3. Create Boolean variables `rev[i][p]` for each student i and play p, indicating whether student i reviews play p.  
4. Add **non-empty constraint**: each student reviews at least one play.

8. Add **exactly-two-pairs-identical constraint**: among the five students, there is exactly one pair of distinct students who review *exactly* the same set of plays; all other students have unique review sets.

   - Since five students and exactly two share a set (i.e., one duplication), then d = 4, and the multiplicity of one value is exactly 2 (others are 1).  
   - Enforce: sum over all values v of `max(0, count(v) - 1)` = 1; equivalently, encode via additional auxiliary variables or use `Distinct` with a single equality pair.  
   - Simpler: enforce existence of exactly one unordered pair (i,j), i<j, such that `set[i] == set[j]`, and for all other pairs (i,k) with k≠j, `set[i] != set[k]`.  
9. Add **exactly-three-undulation constraint**: total number of students reviewing Undulation (play index 2) is exactly three.
