4. Add **O-first-or-fifth constraint**: `pos_O == 1 OR pos_O == 5`.

6. Add **P-before-S constraint**: `pos_P < pos_S`.

8. Add **T-F-R adjacency constraint**: `(pos_T + 1 == pos_F) OR (pos_R + 1 == pos_T)`.  
9. Add **at-least-one-between-O-and-S constraint**: `(pos_O + 2 ≤ pos_S) OR (pos_S + 2 ≤ pos_O)`.
