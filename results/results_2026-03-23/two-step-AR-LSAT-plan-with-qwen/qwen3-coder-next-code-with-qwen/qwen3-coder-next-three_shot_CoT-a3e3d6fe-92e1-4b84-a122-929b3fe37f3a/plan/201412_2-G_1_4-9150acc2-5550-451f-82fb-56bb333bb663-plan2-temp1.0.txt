8. Translate answer choices to role indices: guitar=0, keyboard=1, saxophone=3, trumpet=4, violinist=5.  
9. For each candidate role in the answer choices (excluding 'violinist'? No—include all five), do:

   - Assert `pos[candidate] == 3`.
